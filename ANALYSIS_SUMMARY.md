## Key Findings

### 1. High Coverage Rate
126 out of 132 TXT chapters are successfully mapped to JSON categories. Only 6 chapters (4.5%) have no corresponding JSON category.

### 2. Strategic Organization Shift
- **TXT**: Situational (when-based) → "When waking up", "When entering mosque", "When angry"
- **JSON**: Thematic (purpose-based) → "Salah", "Travel", "Social Interactions", "Protection of Faith"

### 3. Consolidation Pattern
Multiple TXT chapters consolidate into broader JSON categories:
- Social Interactions: 21 TXT chapters → 6 duas
- Protection of Faith: 14 TXT chapters → 6 duas
- Salah (Prayer): 10 TXT chapters → 52 duas
- Travel: 10 TXT chapters → 13 duas

### 4. JSON is a Superset of TXT
The JSON dataset includes 75 "Names of Allah" duas that don't correspond to any TXT chapter. This suggests JSON has been deliberately expanded beyond the original Hisn al-Muslim text.

### 5. Minor Gaps in Specific Domains
- **Financial/Social**: 3 chapters unmapped (66% coverage)
- **Food/Eating**: 2 chapters unmapped (71% coverage)
- **Prayer/Travel**: 100% coverage
